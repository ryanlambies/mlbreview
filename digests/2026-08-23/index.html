Storylines
NYM @ CWS · 1–2 · Walk-Off
The White Sox walked off in the ninth inning as Tristan Peters launched his ninth home run of the season on a fly ball to center field, lifting Chicago to a 2-1 victory over the Mets. The decisive blast came after Andrew Benintendi had doubled in the eighth inning to drive in a run and extend the White Sox's hopes, ultimately setting the stage for Peters's heroics against losing pitcher Kodai Senga.
CIN @ AZ · 3–5 · Comeback
The Arizona Diamondbacks rallied from behind to defeat the Cincinnati Reds 5-3, with the comeback powered by a seventh-inning explosion that proved decisive. Ildemaro Vargas delivered the knockout blow with his fifth triple of the season, a ground ball to center field that scored both Jordan Lawlar and James McCann and shifted the momentum entirely in Arizona's favor. Jonathan Loáisiga earned the win while Kevin Ginkel closed it out for the save, consigning Andrew Abbott to the loss despite his earlier command.
ATH @ HOU · 7–6 · Comeback
The Athletics mounted a comeback victory over the Astros 7-6, with Max Muncy's seventh home run of the season providing the decisive blow in the seventh inning. Houston had jumped out to an early lead on Jose Altuve's fourteenth homer in the first, but Lawrence Butler's fourteenth double in the fifth helped spark Oakland's rally. Jack Perkins earned the win while Hogan Harris notched the save in the one-run thriller.
