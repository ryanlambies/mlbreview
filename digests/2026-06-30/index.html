MIN @ HOU · 4–6 · Comeback
The Houston Astros rallied from an early deficit to defeat the Minnesota Twins 6-4, with Yordan Alvarez delivering the decisive blow in the fourth inning on his 26th home run of the season, a grand slam to right center field that cleared the bases. The Twins had taken an early lead in the first when Ryan Kreidler singled to left, but the Astros' explosive fourth-inning outburst proved insurmountable, aided by a crucial checked-swing walk for Jose Altuve that loaded the bases before Alvarez's blast. Mike Burrows earned the win while Josh Hader recorded the save in Houston's comeback victory.

WSH @ BOS · 8–1
The Washington Nationals dominated the Boston Red Sox with an 8-1 victory, powered by a decisive seventh-inning surge that proved insurmountable. Cade Cavalli earned the win while Greg Weissert took the loss, with the Nationals' offense breaking through in the middle innings as Luis García Jr. doubled in two runs and Keibert Ruiz singled home another to put the game out of reach. The Red Sox managed little against Cavalli, who was supported by his team's timely hitting led by García Jr.'s 15th double of the season.
