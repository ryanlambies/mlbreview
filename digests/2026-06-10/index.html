Storylines
WSH @ SF · 10–11 · Walk-Off
The San Francisco Giants walked off with an 11-10 victory over the Washington Nationals when Bryce Eldridge belted a grand slam to right field in the bottom of the ninth, his fourth home run of the season. The Nationals had taken a lead earlier in the game when James Wood launched his 18th home run in the third inning, but Eldridge's decisive blast off Mitchell Parker proved to be the difference in a thrilling finish.
CHC @ COL · 2–3
TJ Rumfield's eighth home run in the bottom of the eighth inning proved to be the difference as the Colorado Rockies edged the Chicago Cubs 3-2, with Antonio Senzatela earning the win. Ian Happ's fifteenth home run cut it close in the ninth, but Brett Sullivan's late single couldn't complete the Cubs' comeback.
LAD @ PIT · 8–9 · Comeback
The Pittsburgh Pirates staged a dramatic eighth-inning comeback to defeat the Los Angeles Dodgers 9-8, with Tyler Callihan's two-run homer off Kyle Hurt proving to be the decisive blow. The Dodgers had taken control in the sixth when Ryan Ward launched his third home run of the season, a grand slam that brought three runners across, but the Pirates rallied back behind Callihan's heroics and Gregory Soto's save. Evan Sisk earned the win as Pittsburgh erased an early deficit to claim the one-run victory at home.
