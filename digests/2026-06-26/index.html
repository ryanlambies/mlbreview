Storylines
COL @ MIN · 8–9 · Extra Innings
The Minnesota Twins outlasted the Colorado Rockies 9-8 in extra innings, with Andrew Morris earning the win in a back-and-forth contest that extended to the tenth. Hunter Goodman's 22nd home run of the season gave Colorado a brief lead in the ninth, but Byron Buxton answered with a single in the bottom half to help keep Minnesota alive. The Twins ultimately prevailed when Ezequiel Tovar reached on a fielder's choice in the tenth, allowing the winning run to score.
CIN @ PIT · 6–4
The Cincinnati Reds topped the Pittsburgh Pirates 6-4 behind a decisive eighth-inning blast from Noelvi Marte, whose two-run homer on a line drive to left center field proved the difference-maker. Brock Burke earned the win while Caleb Ferguson notched the save, with Mason Montgomery taking the loss after surrendering Marte's fourth home run of the season. The Pirates had briefly threatened in the sixth inning when Esmerlyn Valdez and Marcell Ozuna each connected for home runs off Andrew Abbott, but the Reds' late surge proved too much to overcome.
