MIL @ LAD · 6–2 · Comeback
The Milwaukee Brewers rallied past the Los Angeles Dodgers 6-2 behind a decisive seventh inning explosion led by Jake Bauers, whose two-run homer proved to be the game-winning blow and his 26th of the season. Garrett Mitchell added another fly ball homer in the same inning as the Brewers erased a deficit, with DL Hall earning the win and Trevor Megill notching the save over Blake Snell. The Dodgers had threatened earlier when Will Smith doubled in the sixth to drive in two runs, but it proved insufficient against Milwaukee's late surge.
