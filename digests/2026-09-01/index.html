San Francisco Giants 12, Pittsburgh Pirates 13. Mason Montgomery earned the win. Key moment: Rafael Flores Jr. reaches on a throwing error by third baseman Osleivis Basabe. Brandon Lowe scores..
BAL @ COL · 2–4
The Colorado Rockies edged the Baltimore Orioles 4-2 behind a decisive seventh-inning two-run blast from TJ Rumfield, his 14th home run of the season. Jackson Holliday answered with his seventh homer in the top of the inning to keep the Orioles within striking distance, but it proved too late as Jaden Hill earned the win and Jordan Romano notched the save.
TOR @ CLE · 1–6
The Cleveland Guardians dominated the Toronto Blue Jays 6-1, with Patrick Bailey's two-run homer in the fifth inning serving as the decisive blow. Bailey's eighth home run of the season, hit on a fly ball to center field, proved to be the game-winner as Gavin Williams earned the victory over Spencer Arrighetti. Angel Genao added his second home run of the year in the seventh inning to further pad Cleveland's lead.
