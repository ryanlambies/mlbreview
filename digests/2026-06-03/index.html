PIT @ HOU · 9–11 · Comeback
The Houston Astros mounted a dramatic two-run comeback against the Pittsburgh Pirates, rallying from a significant deficit to win 11-9 despite Henry Davis's devastating fifth home run that cleared the bases in the fourth inning. The turning point came in the eighth when Cam Smith tripled to right field to score two runs, and Isaac Paredes drew a walk during a wild pitch by Gregory Soto that allowed the tying run to score. Josh Hader earned the save as AJ Blubaugh picked up the win for Houston's thrilling comeback victory.

SF @ MIL · 1–0 · Pitchers' Duel
Logan Webb and the San Francisco Giants shut out the Milwaukee Brewers 1-0 in a tight pitchers' duel, with Keaton Winn earning the save by striking out Brice Turang swinging in the bottom of the ninth. The decisive moment came after Christian Yelich had doubled to give the Brewers a glimmer of hope, but Turang couldn't capitalize, leaving Milwaukee's comeback bid incomplete.
