Storylines
KC @ DET · 5–4 · Comeback
The Kansas City Royals rallied past the Detroit Tigers 5-4 in a one-run comeback, with Michael Massey's two-run homer in the eighth inning proving to be the difference. Massey's blast to right field, his eighth home run of the season, broke a tight contest and shifted momentum decisively in Kansas City's favor. Nate Pearson earned the win while Steven Cruz notched the save, with Drew Sommers taking the loss for Detroit.
ATL @ BAL · 3–2 · Extra Innings
The Atlanta Braves outlasted the Baltimore Orioles 3-2 in 11 innings, with Tyler Kinley earning the win and Andrew Kittredge taking the loss. The decisive blow came in the bottom of the 10th when Gunnar Henderson lined into an unassisted double play off Danny Young, a momentum-crushing moment that the Orioles challenged but saw upheld, effectively ending Baltimore's hopes in that inning.
SD @ MIA · 5–3 · Comeback
The San Diego Padres rallied in the eighth inning to defeat the Miami Marlins 5-3, powered by back-to-back home runs off Lake Bachar. Manny Machado's two-run blast to center field, his 23rd of the season, proved decisive, followed immediately by Jackson Merrill's solo shot to right-center for his 15th homer. Wandy Peralta earned the win with Mason Miller closing out the comeback.
