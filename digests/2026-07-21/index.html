ATH @ AZ · 5–6 · Extra Innings
The Arizona Diamondbacks outlasted the Athletics 6-5 in extra innings, with Kevin Ginkel earning the win and Luis Medina taking the loss. Jacob Wilson's seventh home run of the season in the top of the ninth proved costly, as the Athletics took a brief lead before Arizona rallied back to win it in the tenth.
WSH @ COL · 7–8
The Colorado Rockies edged the Washington Nationals 8-7 in a back-and-forth contest decided by Hunter Goodman's 31st home run in the eighth inning, a fly ball to left center field off Clayton Beeter. The Nationals had threatened earlier with CJ Abrams launching his 24th homer in the third inning and James Wood doubling in two runs in the fourth, but Goodman's clutch blast proved the difference as Juan Mejia earned the win and Jordan Romano recorded the save.
