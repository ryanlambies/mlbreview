KC @ LAA · 7–6
The Kansas City Royals edged the Los Angeles Angels 7-6 behind a pivotal fifth-inning two-run homer from Jac Caglianone, his 22nd of the season, which proved to be the game-winner with Alex Lange earning the victory and Steven Cruz securing the save. The Angels had mounted a strong third-inning response when Nolan Schanuel doubled in three runs to briefly swing momentum in their favor, but Caglianone's power stroke ultimately decided the one-run contest.
BAL @ TB · 6–5
The Baltimore Orioles edged the Tampa Bay Rays 6-5, with Josh Walker earning the win and Cam Sanders recording the save. In the second inning, Richie Palacios drove in a run with his 10th double of the season, but the Rays couldn't overcome the deficit despite Junior Caminero's season of 35 home runs and 78 RBIs, as Ryan Vilade flew out to center in the ninth inning for the game-ending out.
