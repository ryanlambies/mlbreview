Storylines
TEX @ SEA · 3–4 · Comeback
The Seattle Mariners rallied past the Texas Rangers 4-3 with Julio Rodríguez's three-run home run in the seventh inning serving as the decisive blow, his 21st of the season on a line drive to left center field. The Rangers had taken an early lead when Joc Pederson homered in the third, but Logan Gilbert earned the win with Andrés Muñoz securing the save despite a ninth-inning challenge from the visitors.
HOU @ PHI · 2–1 · Pitchers' Duel
In a tight pitcher's duel, Cam Smith delivered the decisive blow with his 20th home run of the season, a ninth-inning fly ball to center field off Jhoan Duran that lifted the Houston Astros past the Philadelphia Phillies 2-1. Bryan Abreu earned the win while Josh Hader recorded the save, preserving a thrilling one-run victory for Houston.
TB @ ATL · 1–3
The Atlanta Braves defeated the Tampa Bay Rays 3-1, with the decisive eighth-inning moment coming when Ozzie Albies notched his first triple of the season on a fly ball to center field, sparking the home team's crucial rally. Austin Riley followed with a single that scored Albies, extending the Braves' lead late in the game, while Raisel Iglesias closed out the victory for winning pitcher Didier Fuentes against Kevin Kelly.
